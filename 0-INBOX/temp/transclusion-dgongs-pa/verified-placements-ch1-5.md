## Notes on the ⚑ rows
- **^2-4 → 749.** The verse's content is in the paragraph under 753; 749 is the first of the five "praise of ethics" headings, which 2-4 appears to open. Alternative: before 753.
- **^3-2 → 824.** Paragraph 826 announces the verse ("གལ་ཏེ་ཞེས་སོགས"), paragraph 828 glosses "གནས་མིན". Heading 824 is the nearest announcing heading.
- **^3-3 → 830.** No heading or sa-bcad of its own; 830 begins with a paraphrase of the verse's first line.
- **^1-11 → 640.** No heading of its own; 640 is the paragraph that explains "ལོངས་སྤྱོད་ … སྦྱིན་ལས་འབྱུང་".
